## Part 1 — Unnamed creature types

**90 of 201 already built.** 111 missing. Grouped by how much it matters:

### Recurring/common — show up constantly, no stat block exists yet
These aren't exotic; they're the bread-and-butter NPCs of every Ten-Towns location and would come up almost every session:
- **Commoner**, **Tribal Warrior**, **Scout**, **Priest**, **Acolyte**, **Cultist**, **Cult Fanatic**, **Druid** (plain), **Mage** (plain — we only have "Mage, Frost"), **Bandit Captain**, **Berserker** (plain — distinct from Chardalyn Berserker), **Ghost**
- **Frost Druid** — notably absent despite appearing in 6+ locations (Bremen, Lonelywood, Ch2, Ch5, Ch6) and backing three named NPCs (Faash, Gildreban, Siovakis)

### Correction, not addition
- **Specter** vs **Spectator** — these are two different monsters (Specter = incorporeal undead; Spectator = aberrant eye-tyrant). We only have Spectator. Specter is unbuilt.

### Signature / boss-tier monsters
- **Remorhaz** and **Young Remorhaz** — Ch6 signature monster, flagged as missing in the prior audit and still not built
- **Demilich** — Iriolarthas, the Ch7 final-dungeon villain
- **Arcanaloth** — Scrivenscry, major Ch7 unique
- **Tarrasque** — no stat block exists even though the Scroll of Tarrasque Summoning item does
- **Hydra**, **Tomb Tapper**, **Iron Golem**, **Helmed Horror**, **Gelatinous Cube**, **Living Bigby's Hand**, **Living Blade of Disaster**, **Living Demiplane**, **Telepathic Pentacle** — the full Ch7 Doom of Ythryn unique-construct set, still entirely unbuilt
- **Night Hag** — Auntie Pinch/Pillage/Plunder coven, Ch7

### Undead/lower-tier monsters missing outright

## Part 2 — Named NPCs

**14 of ~150 named individuals already built:** Arveiaturace, Auril (all 3 forms), Avarice, Dzaan's Simulacrum, Bjornhild Solvigsdottir, Isarr Kronenstrom, Nass Lantomir's Ghost, Xardorok Sunblight, Grandolpha Muzgardt, Vellynne Harpell, Gunvald Halraggson, Jarund Elkhardt, Goliath Werebear (Toji = Oyaminartok), Hengar Aesnvaard.

### Low priority — mechanically reuse an existing/missing generic stat block, mostly flavor
Everyone else: Torrga Icevein, Hlin Trollbane, Dannika Graysteel, most town Speakers/innkeepers/shopkeepers (Commoner-tier), Kadroth/Hethyl Arkorran (Cult Fanatic-tier), Nildar Sunblight and the rest of the Sunblight duergar rank-and-file (Dreck/Nefrun/Klondorn/Thontara), Pekoe Quint (reuses Doppelganger), Sikki-kree (reuses Aarakocra), the Akannathi/Thuunlakalaga goliath tribe rosters (reuse Goliath Warrior/Reghed-equivalent stats), Tekeli-li (reuses Gnoll Vampire, which we already have), Ajka/Grava (reuse Reghed Chieftain/Saber-Toothed Tiger, already have both), Mjenir, Krintaas (reuses Wight), Ukuma/Söpo/Iskra/Zelennor/Korgrah (Auril's-island supporting cast, reuse Giant Walrus/Ice Mephit/Roc/Weasel/Abominable Yeti), Wynneth/Froya/King Korold/Ulkora/Beldora (non-combat lore figures).

**F'yorl** was already deliberately skipped per the prior session (CR0, zero attacks, pure roleplay filler) — still correctly excluded.

## Bottom line
The roster covers every **major storyline boss and all of Chapters 1–5's signature content** solidly. The real gaps cluster in two places:
1. **Common recurring NPCs** (Commoner, Tribal Warrior, Scout, Priest, Mage, Cultist, etc.) — needed for basically any town encounter, currently absent entirely.
2. **Chapter 6–7 content** (Caves of Hunger, Doom of Ythryn) — this is the least-covered part of the book: Remorhaz, the drow, the undead roster, and the entire Ythryn construct/chimera/demilich cast are missing.

This is a list only — nothing has been built. Let me know which parts (if any) you want me to start on.
